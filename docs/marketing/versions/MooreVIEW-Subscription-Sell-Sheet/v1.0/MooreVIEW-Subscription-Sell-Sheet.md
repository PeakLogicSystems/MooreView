## What is a Watch Plan?

A **Watch Plan** is a monthly subscription. mooreVIEW watches your site all day and all night.

We send alerts when something starts to go wrong. Your team fixes it on a **planned visit** — not on an emergency call.

**Old way:** Wait until it breaks. Then pay a lot. Then wait in line.

**mooreVIEW way:** See trouble early. Plan the fix. Save money and stress.

## Every Watch Plan includes

| You get | What it means |
|---------|---------------|
| **24/7 watching** | Sensors and software never sleep |
| **Alerts** | Text, email, or app when something drifts |
